    Cardiology Department

    Consistently ranked among Telangana's top cardiac programs for outcomes in heart failure, interventional cardiology, and electrophysiology.

    We offer a full continuum of cardiovascular services — from non-invasive diagnostics like stress testing and echocardiography, to complex interventions like coronary artery bypass grafting and TAVR. Our cardiac rehabilitation program is designed for post-procedure patients across Hyderabad and the twin cities.

    Book Cardiology Appointment

    Neurology Department

    Specialised in brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nervous system disorders — with Telangana's only 24/7 dedicated stroke response unit.

    Conditions treated include stroke, multiple sclerosis, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, dementia, and neuropathies. Our neurosurgery division handles brain tumors, spinal disc herniation, and deep brain stimulation using intraoperative 3T MRI imaging — serving patients from Hyderabad, Warangal, and Nizamabad.

    Book Neurology Appointment

    Hepatology & Gastroenterology

    Expert care for liver disease, digestive disorders, and gastrointestinal conditions — widely trusted across Telangana and Andhra Pradesh.

    Services include advanced endoscopy, colonoscopy, liver biopsy, ERCP, and management of cirrhosis, hepatitis B & C, inflammatory bowel disease, and pancreatic disorders. Our team works closely with regional transplant centers in Hyderabad including KIMS and Yashoda Hospital networks.

    Book Gastroenterology Appointment

    Pediatrics Department

    Child-friendly, family-centred care for infants, children, and adolescents — with Hyderabad's most advanced 20-bed NICU.

    Our board-certified pediatricians and neonatologists cover general pediatric medicine, pediatric cardiology, developmental pediatrics, and adolescent medicine. The department features a colourful, welcoming environment designed to put young patients at ease — with a dedicated parent counselling lounge and 24-hour visiting for mothers.

    Ophthalmology Department

    Comprehensive eye care from routine testing to complex surgical interventions — using the most advanced ophthalmic technology in Hyderabad.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Everything you need to know about visiting Swasthya Hospital

    Does Swasthya Hospital accept Ayushman Bharat / PM-JAY?

    Yes. We are an empanelled hospital under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY, EHS Telangana, CGHS, and most major private insurance schemes including Star Health, HDFC Ergo, New India Assurance, and United India. Please bring your insurance card and Aadhaar for cashless admission.

    What are the OPD timings at the Banjara Hills campus?

    OPD is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, and Sundays from 9:00 AM to 2:00 PM. The Emergency Department operates 24 hours, 7 days a week. The Pharmacy is open until 10:00 PM on weekdays.

    Is there a Tele-Consultation option for patients from Warangal or Vijayawada?

    Absolutely. We offer video consultations with all our senior specialists. Patients from Warangal, Vijayawada, Nalgonda, Karimnagar, and other cities can book tele-consultation through our website or by calling +91 040-6600-1001. Prescriptions are digitally signed and sent via WhatsApp.

    How do I get my lab reports from Swasthya's NABL Lab?

    Lab reports are available on our patient portal within 4-6 hours for routine tests. You will receive an SMS once reports are ready. Critical reports are communicated directly to the treating doctor. Physical copies are available at the lab reception with a valid UHID.

    Does the hospital provide ambulance services in Hyderabad?

    Yes. We operate 8 ALS (Advanced Life Support) ambulances serving Hyderabad, Secunderabad, Cyberabad, and surrounding districts. For ambulance dispatch call our Emergency line: 040-6600-1000. Response time within Banjara Hills and surrounding areas is typically under 15 minutes.
