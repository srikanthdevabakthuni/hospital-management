    Cardiology Department

    Our Cardiology Department is consistently ranked among the top cardiac programs in the region for outcomes in heart failure, interventional cardiology, and electrophysiology.

    We offer a full continuum of cardiovascular services — from non-invasive diagnostics such as stress testing and echocardiography, to complex interventions like coronary artery bypass grafting and transcatheter aortic valve replacement (TAVR). Our cardiac rehabilitation program helps patients recover faster and reduce the risk of future cardiac events through structured exercise, nutrition counseling, and medical monitoring.

    Neurology Department

    Our neurology team specializes in the diagnosis and management of disorders affecting the brain, spinal cord, and peripheral nervous system.

    Conditions treated include stroke, multiple sclerosis, epilepsy, Parkinson's disease, dementia, and neuropathies. We operate a 24/7 stroke response protocol with a dedicated stroke unit, ensuring rapid intervention to minimize neurological damage. Our neurosurgery division handles brain tumors, spinal disc herniation, and deep brain stimulation procedures using state-of-the-art intraoperative imaging and neuronavigation systems.

    Hepatology & Gastroenterology

    Our hepatology and gastroenterology unit provides expert care for liver disease, digestive disorders, and gastrointestinal cancers.

    Services include advanced endoscopy, colonoscopy, liver biopsy, ERCP, and management of conditions such as cirrhosis, hepatitis B and C, inflammatory bowel disease, and pancreatic disorders. Our liver transplant coordination team works closely with regional transplant centers to evaluate and manage patients awaiting or recovering from liver transplantation, providing continuous specialist support throughout the process.

    Pediatrics Department

    Our child-friendly Pediatrics Department provides compassionate, family-centered care for infants, children, and adolescents from birth through age 18.

    Our team of board-certified pediatricians and pediatric subspecialists covers general pediatric medicine, neonatology, pediatric cardiology, developmental pediatrics, and adolescent medicine. The department features a dedicated pediatric ward designed to be welcoming and non-intimidating for young patients, with play areas, colorful decor, and family accommodations — because we know a calm child heals faster.

    Ophthalmology Department

    Our ophthalmology team delivers comprehensive eye care — from routine vision testing to complex surgical interventions — using the most advanced ophthalmic technology available.

    We offer cataract surgery, LASIK and LASEK refractive procedures, glaucoma management, retinal laser therapy, and treatment for conditions including macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and corneal disease. Our optical center provides prescription lenses and contact fittings, and our low vision clinic supports patients with significant vision impairment through assistive devices and lifestyle adaptation programs.
